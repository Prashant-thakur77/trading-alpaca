## 2. What wins, in priority order

1. **A filled multi-leg options order.** The scarcest artifact in the field —
   nobody has shown one. It is also the single most credible proof that
   everything else is real.
2. **A credential-free judge page** where a judge replays a real decision and
   verifies the hash chain themselves. Turns claims into something they *do*.
3. **The calibration loop** — an analyst that was wrong loses its vote. This is
   the Originality axis and no "LLM picks a trade" bot will have it. It must be
   *visible*, not merely implemented.
4. **Abstention shown as prominently as trades.** "We declined 87% of
   opportunities, here is each reason" is a Business Value story almost nobody
   else can tell, and we already have the journal to prove it.

## 3. Hard rules for this plan

- **Every day ends submittable.** If Wednesday explodes, Tuesday's version still
  wins something. This is non-negotiable given a hard deadline with no fixes
  afterwards.
- **No unearned numbers, ever.** We deleted fabricated backtest stats in Phase 1
  and an unearned "1006 trade analyses" claim. Every figure we show is computed
  from real data or it does not appear.
- **Trim depth, never honesty.** If the committee ships with two analysts
  instead of four, we say so.

## 5. What we deliberately are NOT building

Named so nobody re-litigates them at 02:00 on Thursday:

- Full 2-round bull/bear debate (trimmed — calibration is the better story)
- News analyst (cut for time; the API is verified working if slack appears)
- Structure analyst (cut)
- Chart/vision analysis (expensive, low signal, already disabled)
- Self-hosted dashboard (the Artifact judge page is the demo URL)
- Real-time streaming fills (polling works; `TradingStream` is a stretch goal)
- Multi-underlying trading (SPY only keeps the demo legible)

## 6. Known limitations we will state honestly

Judges reward stated limits. These go in the README and the video:

- `consecutive_losses` is inert until exit monitoring lands (Wednesday). The 2%
  daily-loss halt is the live backstop and captures open P&L.
- An assigned equity position freezes the desk into abstention until Wednesday's
  exit monitoring. Fails closed; no money at risk.
- The vega limit binds on straddles, not verticals — the legs' vegas cancel.
- Paper trading only. Small sample. We will not imply statistical significance
  from a handful of trades.
